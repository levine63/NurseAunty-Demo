Painted ORS set for Rwanda, version 1 - how the 24 pictures were asked for

Made 2026-11-11 with Google's Gemini picture model (gemini-nano-banana-2.1), by the method in
ADR-075 (docs/decisions/DECISION_LOG.md):

- Every request carried the master pictures named below as reference pictures: the mother, her
  son, the household things, and for some scenes the community health worker or the father.
- The people were drawn alone on a flat magenta background, cut out, and laid over one stored
  picture of the room, so the room is identical in every picture.
- The finished pictures are 1024 pixels square. The app keeps them as WebP files named
  generated_rw_ors_<number>_<name>_v1.webp in apps/pwa-sandbox/assets/generated/.

Changed 2026-11-12 (ADR-081): pictures 06, 07 and 13 of this set (pour sachet, shake, cover it) showed a
plain water bottle and are no longer in the app. They were redrawn with a jug; the wording for those
three is in rw_ors_mixing_jug_v2.txt. The wording below is kept as it was sent.

They are prototype drafts. Rwandan reviewers check each one (ADR-077): does it look like Rwanda,
and does it show the right action and amount. Known already: the generator drew the boy into
picture 04 although it was asked for the mother alone, and the bottle's one-litre line is not
clearly visible in the mixing pictures.

Wording sent with every request
-------------------------------
Mother: Reference picture A is the MOTHER: keep her face, skin tone, green patterned head wrap, teal top, patterned wrapper skirt and sandals exactly. She is NOT holding a baby; ignore the baby.

Boy: Reference picture B is her SON, about four years old: keep his face, hair, skin tone, green T-shirt, blue shorts and sandals exactly, and keep him clearly small beside an adult: standing, the top of his head is no higher than her waist.

Things: Reference picture C shows the family's HOUSEHOLD THINGS: use these exact things wherever the scene needs them and do not invent different ones.

Group: Draw ONLY the people and the things they are touching or using, complete from head to feet, small enough to leave a clear margin on every side. The background must be one completely flat, plain, bright magenta colour (pure #FF00FF) everywhere: no room, no floor, no wall, no ground line, no shadow, no gradient. Use no pink or purple anywhere on the people or the things. Same warm, clean illustration style and line quality as the reference pictures. No words, letters or numbers anywhere. Square picture.

Used where the scene needs them
-------------------------------
Bright: The boy's eyes are fully open and bright.

Unwell: The boy looks tired and unwell only through his posture and his mouth: shoulders dropped, mouth turned down. His eyes are fully open and looking at his mother, with no dark rings, no sunken eyes and no tears.

Father: Reference picture D is the FATHER: keep his face, hair, shirt, trousers and sandals exactly.
